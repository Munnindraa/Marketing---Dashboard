Key Insights & Recommendations

Data-driven actions for budget optimization
🏆
Google Search Brand Terms — Best ROI
$5.10 CPA with a 5.22% CTR — far above platform average. Brand-intent users convert at highest rate. Recommend increasing budget allocation by 20–30% while search impression share (92%) still has headroom.
⚠️
Google Generic Search — Highest CPA
At $24.80 CPA — 2.5× the portfolio average — Generic Search is the clear underperformer. Review keyword match types, add negatives, and test SKAGs to improve Quality Score from 7 toward 9.
📱
TikTok — Volume Engine, Cost Watch
Drives 50% of all conversions but at a $11.00 CPA and consumes 57% of total spend. Influencer Collab ($9.92 CPA) outperforms Awareness GenZ ($13.00). Shift budget from Awareness to Influencer and Conversion Focus.
🎯
Facebook Retargeting — Hidden Gem
Conversions_Retargeting delivers $5.95 CPA — second only to Google Brand. At only 14% of Facebook's budget, this audience (Cart Abandoners) is underinvested. Scale audience pools and test broader lookalikes.
💡
Facebook — Cheapest Reach, Under-scaled
Facebook has the lowest CPA ($7.64) of any platform yet only 14% of total spend. With CPM at $4.03 vs Google's $5.22, it offers efficient reach. A 30% budget increase could yield ~700 additional conversions at current efficiency.
📈
TikTok Video Completion Funnel
Influencer Collab retains ~33% of viewers to 100% completion vs Awareness_GenZ at ~22%. Higher completion = stronger brand recall. Use top-performing creative hooks from Influencer campaigns in paid Awareness placements.
